# The Inference

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase PostgreSQL and Auth; Vercel-compatible.

## Users
Readers tracking AI news and historical company/model intelligence. Editors managing publication. External agents submitting verified reporting. Primary audience inferred from the brief: developers, researchers and industry decision makers.

## Product Purpose
Make an AI event understandable in seconds, then provide the reporting, primary evidence and historical context needed to inspect it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Database-backed publishing without redeploys. Published-only public access. Protected editorial CMS. Provider-agnostic ingestion defaults to review. Unknown entity specifications stay null. Private commercial repository; no open-source license or contribution workflow.

## Brand Commitments
Temporary name selected under user delegation: The Inference. Premium newspaper × financial terminal × AI research. Bloomberg/Reuters level of clarity and density. Monochrome, sharp separators, deliberate light/dark and mobile reading. No giant marketing hero, glowing AI imagery or rounded-card grid.

## Evidence on Hand
No preexisting reporting or media supplied. Production reporting must be sourced and stored in Supabase. Test fixtures must be clearly synthetic and isolated from production.

## Product Principles
Trace claims to sources. Explain the change first. Preserve history. Separate editorial judgment from automated discovery. Publish through data, never code.
